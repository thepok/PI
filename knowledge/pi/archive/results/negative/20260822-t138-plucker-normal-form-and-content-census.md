## Fixed-Q minor normalization

Status: `proof sketch`

At four checkpoints, index the eight selected phase rows by `a` and put

\[
r_a=(Q_a,z_a),\qquad
P_{a,b}=Q_az_b-Q_bz_a.
\]

Within either phase pair,

\[
Q_{m,0}=10Q_{m,-1}+3,
\qquad 3\nmid Q_{m,-1},
\]

so `gcd(Q_(m,-1),Q_(m,0))=1`. In particular the numeric eight-vector
`Q` is primitive at every registered instance.

Fix one such numeric `Q`. Choose `u` in `Z^8` with `u dot Q=1` and define

\[
\xi_b=\sum_a u_aP_{a,b}=z_b-Q_b(u\mathbin\cdot z).
\]

Then

\[
P_{a,b}=Q_a\xi_b-Q_b\xi_a,
\qquad u\mathbin\cdot\xi=0. \tag{1}
\]

Equivalently, a unimodular integer change of row coordinates sends the fixed
`Q` to `(1,0,...,0)`. In that basis the only potentially nonzero minors are

\[
P'_{1,j}=z'_j,\qquad 2\le j\le8,
\]

and the remaining seven coordinates are algebraically independent. The
pointwise minor algebra is therefore torsion-free. Its fixed-Q relation ideal
is generated by

\[
Q_aP_{b,c}-Q_bP_{a,c}+Q_cP_{a,b}=0; \tag{2}
\]

the usual quadratic Plucker relations follow after this normalization.

For a fixed numeric `Q`, a degree-at-most-two candidate consequently reduces
to either a zero universal identity or a polynomial in seven translated carry
coordinates. A zero normal form cannot meet a strict `0<|H|` requirement. A
nonzero primitive normal form has no nonunit divisor forced merely by
coefficient torsion in the minor geometry; a claimed divisor must use an
additional canonical congruence on the evaluated translated carries.

### Required limitations

This is a pointwise theorem after specializing `Q`. Over the task's symbolic
coefficient ring with indeterminate `Q`, there need not be a polynomial Bezout
vector or one uniform unimodular transformation. The pointwise argument alone
does not generate a symbolic STOP for the frozen uniform degree-two class.

Torsion-free primitive coefficient content also does not rule out a fixed
divisor of integer values: a primitive polynomial such as `X(X-1)` is always
even. Thus (1)--(2) exclude only divisors attributed solely to Plucker
coefficient torsion or common symbolic content. They do not exclude
integer-valued fixed divisors or separately proved canonical congruences.

The natural raw statistic

\[
g=\gcd_{a<b}|P_{a,b}|
\]

is the second determinantal divisor of the `2 x 8` matrix with rows `Q,z`,
because the first determinantal divisor is one. It is not structurally forced
to be nonunit.
