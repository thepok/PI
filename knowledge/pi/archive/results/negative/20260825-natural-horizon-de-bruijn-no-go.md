## Generic endpoint obstruction

Let `k>=2`, `q=10^k`, and let `d_1 d_2 ...` be any infinite decimal word. If
the `q` overlapping length-`k` blocks beginning at positions `0,...,q-1`
contain every length-`k` word, then each word occurs exactly once. Viewed as
the edges of the order-`k` decimal de Bruijn graph, these blocks form an Euler
trail. Every vertex has equal indegree and outdegree, so the trail's initial
and terminal vertices coincide. Hence

\[
d_1\cdots d_{k-1}=d_{q+1}\cdots d_{q+k-1}.
\]

This argument is a `proof sketch`. For the certified π prefix at `k=3`, the
necessary equality would be `14=38`, which fails. The direct scan additionally
identifies the missed target `A=2`.
