T70 does **not** prove V1 unconditionally.  It proves that the following
explicit package is sufficient:

1. \(\mu\) is a probability measure on the circle;
2. \(\mu\) is ergodic for multiplication by 10;
3. \(\operatorname{supp}\mu\subseteq K_\pi\), or equivalently for the supplied
   convenience theorem, \(\mu(K_\pi)=1\);
4. \(\mu\) is not mutually singular with its multiplication-by-16 pushforward;
5. one supplied point \(z\in\operatorname{supp}\mu\) has dense forward orbit
   under the semigroup generated by 10 and 16.

Under those hypotheses T70 proves, in order,

\[
 (T_{16})_*\mu=\mu,
 \qquad \operatorname{supp}\mu=\mathbb T,
 \qquad K_\pi=\mathbb T,
 \qquad \mathrm{V1}.
\]

The 2026-08-13 extension also proves two strictly weaker interfaces.  Under
the explicit source-shaped `FurstenbergSourcePremise` from T77, an infinite
compact circle set forward invariant under multiplication by 10 and 16 is the
whole circle.  Consequently either of the following packages implies V1:

1. a probability measure supported in the pi orbit closure, ergodic under
   multiplication by 10, with infinite support, and not mutually singular
   with its multiplication-by-16 pushforward; or
2. any measure supported in the pi orbit closure, invariant under
   multiplication by 10, with infinite support, whose multiplication-by-16
   pushforward is absolutely continuous with respect to it.

T70 constructs no inhabitant of `FurstenbergSourcePremise`, and it does not
prove infinite support, matching, absolute continuity, ergodicity, or
nonsingularity for a pi empirical limit.  Canonical V1 therefore remains a
`conjecture`.

   \(\operatorname{supp}\mu\) into itself.  The proof tests an open

   \(10^s16^t z\) in the support for every \(s,t\ge0\).

## Source boundary

The informal argument obtains such a point from nonatomicity and
Furstenberg's theorem: a nonatomic probability gives zero mass to the
countable rational circle points, so its support contains an irrational, and
Furstenberg makes that point's \(\{10^s16^t\}\)-orbit dense.  The repository
does not currently formalize that source theorem as a proved Lean result.

The original T70 interface therefore retains the exact supplied-point/dense-
orbit hypothesis.  The extension consumes T77's exact source-shaped premise
as an ordinary theorem argument rather than introducing Furstenberg as an
axiom or disguising it in a definition.  No term of that structure is built
in T70.  For the fixed pi orbit, the remaining bridge is now the weaker
package

\[
 \text{source premise plus infinite support plus common forward invariance}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{supp}\mu=\mathbb T.
\]

The source premise is external mathematical input, and no actual empirical
limit of pi has yet been shown to have the required infinite support and
times-16 support invariance.
