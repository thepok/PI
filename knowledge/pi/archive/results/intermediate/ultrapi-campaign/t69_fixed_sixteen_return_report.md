## Outcome and exact scope

[`T69T69FixedSixteenReturn.lean`](../../TheoryLib/PiQuantitativeBlockHitting/T69T69FixedSixteenReturn.lean)
is a `machine-checked` reduction, not a proof of canonical V1. It defines the
single fixed cross-base return

\[
 16\{\pi\}\pmod 1\in
 \overline{\{\,10^n\pi\pmod 1:n\ge0\,\}}
 \tag{R16}
\]

and proves its exact metric sequential form. It then proves:

1. (R16) makes the closed decimal orbit forward invariant under
   multiplication by \(16\);
2. hence the full forward semigroup orbit
   \(\{10^s16^t\pi\pmod1:s,t\ge0\}\) lies in that closure;
3. assuming exactly that the displayed times-\(10\)/times-\(16\) orbit of
   \(\pi\) is dense, (R16) makes the decimal orbit closure the whole circle;
4. a full circle-orbit closure hits the explicit inner ball of every decimal
   word cylinder and therefore implies the exact list-valued V1 statement;
5. conversely, V1 itself implies (R16), without any Furstenberg premise.

Thus the final theorem is the conditional equivalence

\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{Dense}\{10^s16^t\pi\pmod1:s,t\ge0\}\Longrightarrow
   (\mathrm{V1}\iff\mathrm{R16}).}
\]

This density is the minimal topological premise used by the proof.
Furstenberg's 1967 Theorem IV.1 supplies it on paper because the semigroup
generated by 10 and 16 is nonlacunary and \(\pi\) is irrational. T69 does not
introduce that source theorem as an axiom or machine-formalize it. Therefore
only the displayed implication is `machine-checked`; an unconditional
equivalence is not claimed.

## Exact remaining bottleneck

T69 turns a diffuse disjunctivity question into one fixed-point recurrence
question, but it does not prove that recurrence. The still-missing statement
is exactly

\[
 \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists n\ge0:\quad
 \operatorname{dist}_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}
   (16\pi,10^n\pi)<\varepsilon.
\]

Equivalently, some subsequence of \(10^n\pi\pmod1\) must converge to
\(16\pi\pmod1\). No known irrationality-measure estimate, denominator
valuation, finite digit computation, or almost-everywhere theorem supplied in
the current project proves this fixed return. Canonical V1 therefore remains
a `conjecture`; T69 is not a `candidate resolution` or a `verified
resolution`.
