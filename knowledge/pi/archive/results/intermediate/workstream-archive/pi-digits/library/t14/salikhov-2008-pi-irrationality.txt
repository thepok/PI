                              On the irrationality measure of π

                                                 V. Kh. Salikhov

   In 1953 Mahler [1] obtained the first estimate from below for the order of approximation
of π by rational numbers: |π − p/q| ⩾ q −30 for all p, q ∈ N with q ⩾ q0 .
   This result was repeatedly improved later: Mignotte [2] replaced the exponent 30 by 20,
and Chudnovsky [3] replaced it by 19.8899944 . . . . In a series of papers Hata [4]–[6]
decreased the exponent to 8.016045 . . . . The aim of this work is to prove the following
statement.
Theorem 1. For all p, q ∈ N with q ⩾ q0 the following inequality holds:
