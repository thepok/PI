                                                             Short Kloosterman sums to powerful modulus

                                          The number q is called powerful if its kernel d = p|q p is small relative to q in the
                                          logarithmic scale. The simplest case of such numbers are q = pn where p is fixed prime
                                          and n → +∞.
                                              For many times, A.A. Karatsuba [1]-[3] pointed out to the possibility of estimating
                                          of such sums with q = pn by method of A.G. Postnikov [4], [5]. In this paper, we prove
                                          the following statement.
