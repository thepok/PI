                                                   phantine conditions on ξ and α, we obtain lower bounds on the number of occurrences for which the
                                                   fractional part of the sequence (ξαn )n≥1 falls into a prescribed region I ⊂ [0, 1], extending several

                                                   measures is logarithmic, generalizing a recent result of Varjú and Yu.

   There is currently much interest in the theory of Fourier transforms of self-similar measures, see
the definition in Section 4. Denote the reciprocal of the contraction ratio of self-similar measure
by α, we know that the Fourier analytic properties of self-similar measures have close connections
with the distribution of fractional part of the sequence (ξαn )n≥1 , see for example [7, 11, 22]; for
more recent results, see [2, 21, 24]. We also refer to a recent survey by Sahlsten [23].
   In this paper, we prove some results on the distribution of fractional parts of the sequence (ξαn )
under various Diophantine conditions on ξ and α. We then apply our results to study the Fourier
decay of self-similar measures in Section 4. In particular, we employ these new results to obtain a
generalization of a recent result of Varjú and Yu [24]; see Theorem 4.2 for a precise statement.

Lemma 3.1. Let ξ, α, η be fixed real numbers. Let 0 < δ < 1 be a constant. Suppose that there
exist constants γ, γ0 > 0 such that
          sup{k : ∥ξαn − η∥ < δ, ∥ξαn+1 − η∥ < δ, . . . , ∥ξαn+k − η∥ < δ} < γn + γ0

Proof. Let X = {n ∈ N : ∥ξαn − η∥ ≥ δ} and label the elements in X in increasing order by
x1 , x2 , · · · . Let x0 = 0. By the assumption, for each m ≥ 0, we have
                xm+1 ≤ xm + γ(xm + 1) + γ0 + 2 = xm (1 + γ) + (γ + γ0 + 2).
It follows that xm+1 +B ≤ (1+γ)(xm +B), where B = (γ +γ0 +2)/γ. Thus, xm +B ≤ B(1+γ)m
