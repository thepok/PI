## S1 Singh--Venkataramana

ArXiv page 1 defines

> D = (theta + beta_1 - 1)...(theta + beta_n - 1)
>     - z(theta + alpha_1)...(theta + alpha_n).

ArXiv page 2 states that the monodromy actions of `h_0` and `h_infinity` are
the companion matrices `A` and `B^{-1}`, and `h_1` acts by `C=A^{-1}B`.

ArXiv page 4 states: "Theorem 1.1 holds even when n=2."

Printed page 596 / arXiv page 6, Corollary 1.3:

> The hypergeometric group Gamma subset Sp_4(Z) corresponding to the
> polynomials f=(X-1)^4 and g=(X^2-X+1)^2 is an arithmetic group.

ArXiv page 15 gives `f-g=-2X^3+3X^2-2X` and parameters
`alpha=(0,0,0,0)`, `beta=(1/6,1/6,5/6,5/6)`.

## S2 Salehi Golsefidy--Varju

ArXiv page 2, Theorem 1:

> Let Gamma subset GL_d(Z[1/q_0]) be the group generated by a symmetric set
> S. Then the congruence Cayley graphs form a family of expanders when q
> ranges over square-free integers coprime to q_0 if and only if the connected
> component of the Zariski-closure of Gamma is perfect.

## S3 Bourgain--Varju

ArXiv pages 1--2, Theorem 1:

> Let S subset SL_d(Z) be finite and symmetric. Assume S generates a subgroup
> G which is Zariski dense in SL_d. Then the Cayley graphs of pi_q(G) form a
> family of expanders when q runs through the integers. Moreover, there is
> q_0 such that pi_q(G)=SL_d(Z/qZ) if q is prime to q_0.

ArXiv pages 4--5 define the convolution operator and state the equivalent
uniform nontrivial spectral bound; equation (3) is the trace/L2 estimate used
in the proof.

## S4 Bourgain--Chang

Printed page 38 defines

> q = product p_alpha^{nu_alpha} has few prime factors if
> sum_alpha nu_alpha < C_0 for some constant C_0.

Printed pages 41--42, Corollary 4.5, assumes

> ord_p(theta) > q^delta for all p dividing q, and t>q^delta,

and concludes a bound `< t^{1-epsilon}` for primitive frequencies.

Printed page 43, Theorem 4.7, assumes `|H|=q^delta` and gives
`max |sum_{x in H} e_q(xi x)| < q^{-epsilon}|H|`, with epsilon depending on
the number of prime factors and delta.

## S5 Erdelyi

Printed pages 358--359 define

> P_0(z)=Q_0(z)=1,
> P_{n+1}(z)=P_n(z)+z^{2^n}Q_n(z),
> Q_{n+1}(z)=P_n(z)-z^{2^n}Q_n(z).

Printed page 359, equation (1.1), states for `z` on the unit circle

> |P_n(z)|^2+|Q_n(z)|^2=2^{n+1}=2N, where N=2^n.

Printed page 360, Theorem 2.1, states an absolute lower Mahler-measure bound
and notes that `c_1=e^{-227}` is admissible.

## S7 Konieczny

Printed page 1898 records Gelfond's estimate

> sup_alpha |sum_{n<N} t(n)e(alpha n)| = O(N^{log 3/log 4}).

Printed page 1899, Theorem A, states that for every fixed `s` there is
`c(s)>0` with the Thue--Morse `U^s[N]` norm `O(N^{-c(s)})`.

Printed page 1900, Theorem B, states the analogous fixed-order result for the
Rudin--Shapiro sequence.

## S8 Baker--Banaji

Preprint page 4, Theorem 1.2:

> Let {phi_a:[0,1]->[0,1]} be an IFS of analytic maps and suppose at least one
> phi_a is not affine. Then every self-conformal measure mu has constants
> C,eta>0 such that |mu-hat(xi)| <= C|xi|^{-eta} for every xi nonzero.
