      THEOREM 1 A, B, C.    T is strongly mixing.

     Theorem 1A is well known. It holds even for a compact con-

     This paper was already typed when Professor Krickeberg in a

showed that a wide class of transformations are—what he calls—exact

which implies strongly mixing. Since the proof I give is different
from Rohlin's proof and since Theorem 1 is a straightforward applica-
tion of some lemmas used to prove Theorem 2, I did not withdraw
Theorem 1. Furthermore its proof can be easily extended to show
the mixing property of every degree.
