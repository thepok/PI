## Exact source content used

For indicators `I_i` with probabilities `p_i`, independent marks `U_i`, and
neighborhoods `A_i` containing `i`, the source defines

```text
M=sum_i I_i delta_(U_i),
lambda_measure=sum_i p_i Law(U_i),
lambda=lambda_measure(S)=sum_i p_i,
V_i=sum_(j notin A_i) I_j.
```

Theorem 4.1 states

```text
d2(Law(M),Po(lambda_measure))
 <= E sum_i sum_(j in A_i\{i})
       (5/lambda+3/(V_i+1)) I_i I_j
    + min(epsilon_1,epsilon_2)
    + sum_i sum_(j in A_i)
       (5/lambda+E[3/(V_i+1)|I_j=1]) p_i p_j.
```

Its first alternative error is

```text
epsilon_1=min(1,1.65*lambda^(-1/2))
          *sum_i E|E(I_i | I_j, j notin A_i)-p_i|.
```

For completeness, `epsilon_2` uses a same-space Palm coupling
`Law((J_ji)_j)=Law((I_j)_j|I_i=1)` and is

```text
E sum_i (5/lambda+3/(V_i min sum_(j notin A_i)J_ji+1))
          *sum_(j notin A_i)|J_ji-I_j| p_i.
```

T159 uses `epsilon_1=0`, proved from disjoint digit support; it does not assert
or construct the optional `epsilon_2` coupling. Remark 4.2 records mark-law
independence of the bound under the matching mean measure. Remark 4.3 explains
lifting to `S x I` to make a nonsimple process simple and neighborhoods
determinable.

The source's `d2` is the bounded second Wasserstein pseudometric generated by
its configuration pseudometric `rho_1`; it is not standard quadratic `W_2`.
