                                   3. An obvious flaw
   The equality in (3) is incorrect when 2n + 1 > d − k, since the left-hand side
in the latter case has the denominator 52n+1−d+k m, while the denominator of the
right-hand side is m. Using a shorter finite sum over n to meet this constraint is
not an option either, because there is no bound for (the fractional part of) the tail
in such cases. Are there more suitable formulae for π or other interesting constants,
defined over quadratic or more general algebraic extensions, that could be of use?
