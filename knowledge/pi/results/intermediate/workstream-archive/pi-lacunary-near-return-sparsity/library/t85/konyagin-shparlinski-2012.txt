        ON THE CONSECUTIVE POWERS OF A PRIMITIVE
            ROOT: GAPS AND EXPONENTIAL SUMS


              SERGEI V. KONYAGIN AND IGOR E. SHPARLINSKI


   Abstract. For a primitive root g modulo a prime p ≥ 1 we obtain upper bounds
on the gaps between the residues modulo p of the N consecutive powers ag n ,
n = 1, . . . , N , which is uniform over all integers a with gcd(a, p) = 1.

    §1. Introduction. For a prime p we denote by F p the finite field of
p elements.
    Given a primitive root g ∈ F∗p and an integer N ≥ 1 we denote by Hg, p (N )
the largest gaps between the residues modulo p of the N consecutive powers
ag n , n = 1, . . . , N , (taken over all a ∈ F∗p ). That is, denoting by Gg, p (N ) ⊆ F p
the set of residues of g, . . . , g N modulo p, we have
          Hg, p (N ) = max{H : ∃a ∈ F∗p , ∃u ∈ F p
                       such that {u + 1, . . . , u + H } ∩ a · Gg, p (N ) = ∅}.
In the case where N = t is the multiplicative order of g modulo an integer m ≥ 2,
non-trivial bounds on the similar quantity have been obtained in [4, 5]; see
also [1, 3, 12] for some applications.
    One can immediately estimate Hg, p (N ) directly via the bound of Bourgain
and Garaev [2, Corollary 1.2] on exponential sums
