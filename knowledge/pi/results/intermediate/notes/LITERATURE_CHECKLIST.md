# Literature and attribution checklist

- Locate the original Erdős source, not only the database summary.
- Search exact wording and mathematically equivalent terminology.
- Search MathSciNet, zbMATH, arXiv, Google Scholar, and citation chains when available.
- Check the Erdős Problems discussion and revision history.
- Record results that imply the claim even if they use different notation.
- Distinguish rediscovery, a new proof of a known theorem, a stronger bound, and a resolution.
- Preserve links, bibliographic metadata, query strings, and search dates.
- Ask a specialist before making a public novelty claim.
