# A finite-recurrence criterion for decimal occurrence

Status: **proof sketch**. Independent mathematical review and coordinator
inspection completed; not proof-assistant verified. No novelty claim, no
conclusion for π. This is a foundation extending the feedback examples to
signed coefficients and repeated reduced-denominator cancellations.

## Statement and finite input

Let a_n be rational with rational generating function P(z)/Q(z) in reduced
form, Q(0)=1. Finite initial transients are allowed. Write

    χ(T) = T^r Q(1/T) = T^r − Σ_{j<r} c_j T^j,
    R_p = max(1, max_{j<r} |c_j|_p^(1/(r−j))),
    Γ = ∏_{p≠2,5} R_p.

For r=0 set Γ=1. The absolute value is normalized by |p|_p=p^−1.
Only finitely many factors differ from one. These data come from the minimal
eventual recurrence, not an arbitrary annihilating recurrence. Rational
valuation exponents make comparison with any rational threshold an exact
finite calculation after taking a common positive integer power.

For an integer D≥2 and rational β, put

    x = β + Σ_{n≥0} a_n 10^(−D^n).

If **1<Γ<D²**, every finite decimal word occurs infinitely often in x.
More precisely, there are arithmetic-selected windows on which the decimal
shift empirical measures converge to Lebesgue measure. Consequently
liminf_m ||10^m x||=0, where ||·|| is distance to the nearest integer.
This is not a normality or global positive-frequency assertion.

## Denominator growth and activation

Recurrence induction, including enough initial terms to cover the transient,
gives |a_n|_p≤C_p R_p^n. Only finitely many denominator primes occur.
An analogous real bound is |a_n|≤C Λ^n for some Λ>1. For the partial sum x_N,

    |x−x_N| ≤ 2 C Λ^(N+1) 10^(−D^(N+1))

for sufficiently large N. Choose fixed integers κ,κ₀≥0 clearing the 2- and
5-adic coefficient growth and β, and put m_N=D^N+κN+κ₀. In reduced form,

    10^m_N x_N = A_N/q_N,    gcd(q_N,10)=gcd(A_N,q_N)=1,
    q_N ≤ C₀ Γ^N.

All q_N have prime support in one finite set S disjoint from {2,5}.

The strict lower condition Γ>1 forces q_N to be unbounded, not necessarily
to tend to infinity. Here is a cancellation-safe activation argument.
For some p≠2,5 a coefficient of χ is not p-integral. If all a_n were
p-adically bounded, choose n_* after which the eventual recurrence holds
and scale the tail states (a_n,…,a_{n+r−1}), n≥n_*, into
Z_(p)^r, where Z_(p) is the localization of Z at p. Their generated module
is a finite free module over this principal ideal domain. Minimality of
the eventual recurrence makes its rank r. It is stable under the companion
matrix, so that matrix has p-integral characteristic polynomial in a module
basis, contradicting the coefficient of χ. Thus |a_n|_p is unbounded.
If all q_N were bounded, |x_N|_p would be bounded, whereas
x_N−x_(N−1)=a_N 10^(−D^N) would imply bounded |a_N|_p. This contradiction
proves activation. Select record indices N_k with q_(N_k)→∞.

## Uniform orbit estimate with fixed prime support

For odd q coprime to 10, let

    T₀ = ord_rad(q)(10),
    c(q) = ∏_{p^e || q} p^min(e,v_p(10^T₀−1)),
    L(q) = q/c(q).

Odd-prime lifting of exponents and the Chinese remainder theorem give
ord_q(10)=T₀ L(q). The subgroup generated by 10^T₀ is exactly the units
congruent to 1 modulo c(q), a complete additive grid of length L(q).
For fixed S, c(q) divides a constant C_S: take T_S=lcm_{p∈S}ord_p(10)
and C_S=∏_{p∈S}p^v_p(10^T_S−1). Hence L(q)≥q/C_S.

For any unit A modulo q, integer J≥1, and 1≤h<L(q),

    |Σ_{j<J} exp(2πi h A 10^j/q)| ≤ √q (1+log q).

Proof mechanism: first take h coprime to q. The additive sum over each
complete grid vanishes when L(q)>1. Every character of the subgroup
<10> extends to the full unit group. The Gauss sum of any such extension,
including imprimitive characters, at a unit additive frequency has modulus
at most √q: Parseval gives total squared modulus q φ(q), and all φ(q)
unit frequencies have the same modulus. Averaging extensions bounds each
nonzero time-Fourier coefficient by √q. Fourier inversion and the geometric
sum bound give the displayed logarithmic completion estimate, with complete
periods contributing zero. For general h, reduce to q'=q/gcd(h,q).
Then c(q') divides c(q), and L(q')≥L(q)/gcd(h,q)>1; the same argument applies.
All starting phases are allowed. No distribution hypothesis is assumed.

## Tracking and passage to words

Take J_N=floor((D−1)D^N/2). Uniformly for 0≤j<J_N, circular distance between
10^(m_N+j)x and 10^(m_N+j)x_N is at most

    ε_N = 2 C Λ^(N+1) 10^(−(D−1)D^N/2+κN+κ₀) → 0.

The normalized orbit error is bounded by

    √q_N (1+log q_N)/J_N = O((N+1)(√Γ/D)^N) → 0.

Along N_k, L(q_(N_k))→∞. Fix a nonzero Fourier frequency h first; the orbit
bound eventually applies, and transfer to x adds at most 2π|h|ε_N.
Thus every fixed nonconstant Fourier average tends to zero. Approximate
continuous functions by Fejér polynomials and bracket interval indicators
by continuous boundary ramps. This proves interval convergence; a finite
endpoint grid then proves uniform interval discrepancy convergence.

A word of length ℓ and integer value b corresponds to the half-open cylinder
[b/10^ℓ,(b+1)/10^ℓ). Its positive limiting window frequency supplies an
occurrence in each sufficiently late selected window. Since m_(N_k)→∞,
the occurrences have unbounded positions. The middle half of the cylinder
also supplies a boundary-safe proof independent of decimal endpoint choices.

## What has, and has not, been obtained

The arithmetic condition yields the decisive comparison √Γ<D; it is not
digit distribution renamed. Signed coefficients, finite transients, rational
β, and repeated denominator collapse are covered. The record selection has
no quantitative growth rate from this proof. Equality Γ=D² is untreated.
Examples outside this note require separate checks; no extra example from
the originating research response is endorsed here.

The multiplicative-orbit mechanism is classical; see the comparison already
recorded in [the feedback note](20260907-polynomial-feedback-digits.md).
Priority for this exact formulation has not been established. Moving toward
π requires controlling genuinely growing prime support and retaining a long
tracking window for actual π, neither of which is proved by this criterion.
