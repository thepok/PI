# T149: finite fixed-rho repeated-difference energy scan

Status: `experiment`  
Last audited: 2026-08-22

## Frozen test

For every odd prime `503<p<65536`, define the P21 objects

```text
D_p=max{d:d^3<=p},
I_p=[ceil(p/11),floor(2p/5)],
a_j=[z^j](z^2+2z+2)^j,
b_n=sum_(r=0)^n a_r*a_(n-r),
Z_p={j in I_p:p|a_j},
E_p=sum_(h>=1) max(#{j:j,j+h in Z_p}-1,0).
```

The range and the frozen P21 inequality `E_p^4<=p^3` were fixed before the
run.  Coefficients are generated by the exact modular recurrence

```text
(j+1)a_(j+1)=2(2j+1)a_j+4j*a_(j-1),
```

and the short-convolution hypothesis is tested using
`b_n=4b_(n-1)+4b_(n-2)`.  All indices remain below the characteristic, so
every modular inverse used by the recurrence exists.

## Exact result

The scan checked `6446` primes.  The short-convolution hypothesis failed at
exactly

```text
5741, 8297, 29201, 33461, 45697.
```

Among all other primes:

- no violation of `E_p^4<=p^3` occurred;
- in fact no positive `E_p` occurred at all;
- the distribution of `|Z_p|` was
  `{0:4690,1:1475,2:251,3:21,4:4}`;
- the maximum `|Z_p|=4` occurred exactly at
  `p=5683,37273,49727,57713`, with `E_p=0` in every case.

The corresponding zero-index lists are retained as assertions in the
reproducer.  Thus every observed fixed-rho zero set is Sidon, even though the
T148 universal-root certificate proves that Sidon behavior is false for
arbitrary high-order torsion roots.  This finite distinction supports the
fixed-rho pivot but proves no asymptotic bound.

## Exponent cutoff

The exact comparison needed after a hypothetical P21 proof is elementary.
Writing `x=p^(1/4)`,

```text
p^(3/4)<21p/110-sqrt(p)/2
```

is equivalent to `21x^2-110x-55>0`.  Hence it holds for every prime
`p>=1061`.  Therefore a proved P21 upper bound would contradict the T148
linear lower bound whenever `|Z_p|>=ceil(sqrt(p))` for all such primes.  This
cutoff calculation does not prove the P21 upper bound.
