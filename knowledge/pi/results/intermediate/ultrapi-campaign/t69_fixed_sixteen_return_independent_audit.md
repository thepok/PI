The paper source behind the explicit density premise is H. Furstenberg,
*Disjointness in ergodic theory, minimal sets, and a problem in Diophantine
approximation*, **Mathematical Systems Theory** 1 (1967), 1--49, DOI
[`10.1007/BF01692494`](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01692494). The pinned PDF and

Theorem IV.1 states that a nonlacunary integer semigroup has dense orbit on
every irrational circle point. The semigroup generated by 10 and 16 is
nonlacunary: if both generators were powers of one integer, the prime factor
5 in 10 would also have to occur in 16. Pi is irrational. Allowing exponent
zero in T77's `tenSixteenOrbit` only adds the identity point and cannot destroy
density. These observations explain the paper implication, but T69 correctly
keeps the resulting density as a Lean premise rather than introducing the
paper theorem as an axiom.

## Exact statement and quantifier audit

`FixedSixteenReturn` is definitionally

```text
circleMul 16 (piCircleOrbit 0) ∈ closure (range piCircleOrbit).
```

Here `piCircleOrbit n` is the class of `10^n * Real.pi` in
`UnitAddCircle = R/Z`. Its metric theorem has the correct full closure
quantifiers:

```text
forall epsilon : R, 0 < epsilon ->
  exists n : N,
    dist (circleMul 16 (piCircleOrbit 0)) (piCircleOrbit n) < epsilon.
```

There is no finite cutoff, hidden subsequence, rate hypothesis, or restriction
to positive `n`; `n = 0` is allowed. T77's semigroup orbit has the exact form

```text
{ circleMul (10^s * 16^t) x | s,t : N }.
```

The density premise is specialized to `x = piCircleOrbit 0`; it has no hidden
universal point quantifier or extra invariant-set hypothesis. The conclusion
is the canonical list-valued `Theory.PiDigits.V1`, quantified over every
`List (Fin 10)`, including the empty word. The empty-word case is handled
without an exception because its inner cylinder radius is positive and the
final digit condition is vacuous.

## Proof-by-proof semantic audit

1. `fixedSixteenReturn_iff_metric` unfolds the orbit closure and applies the
   metric closure-of-range characterization. Its distance orientation is
   exact.
2. `piCircleOrbit_eq_circleMul_powTen` proves on `R/Z` that the `n`th decimal
   orbit point is `10^n` times the initial point. The natural/real casts and
   the `n = 0` case reduce correctly.
3. `piOrbitClosure_timesSixteen_mapsTo` first checks the map on every range
   point. Commutativity changes `16 * 10^n` to `10^n * 16`, after which the
   already checked forward-times-ten invariance carries the assumed return
   through all decimal iterates. Continuity of multiplication by 16 and
   closedness of the target extend this map to the closure.
4. `tenSixteenOrbit_pi_subset_piOrbitClosure` iterates the newly proved
   times-16 invariance and the existing times-ten invariance. Both induction
   bases and the multiplication order agree with `10^s * 16^t`.
5. `fixedSixteenReturn_implies_piOrbitClosure_eq_univ` now consumes the exact
   dense joint-orbit premise directly. The joint orbit lies in the closed
   decimal-orbit closure, so closure minimality and density force that closure
   to be all of `R/Z`. No other source premise is used.
6. `piOrbitClosure_eq_univ_implies_v1` chooses the center of each word's open
   inner decimal cylinder, uses full orbit closure to hit its positive-radius
   ball, and applies the endpoint-safe block-match lemma. This produces the
   exact `forall i < w.length` V1 digits, not merely a closed-cylinder hit.
7. `v1_implies_fixedSixteenReturn` uses the already checked
   `v1_iff_pi_baseTenOrbitDense` theorem. It targets the canonical
   representative of `16 * pi` in `[0,1)`, then uses the quotient norm bound
   to turn real fractional-part proximity into circle distance. This
   direction has no joint-orbit-density premise.
8. `v1_iff_fixedSixteenReturn` composes exactly the preceding two directions.
   The reverse direction retains `hdense` explicitly; no proof term for it is
   synthesized or hidden.

## Remaining mathematical obligation

The reduction isolates rather than discharges the fixed-pi obstacle. A
complete proof still requires the unconditional recurrence

\[
  \forall\varepsilon>0\;\exists n\in\mathbb N:\quad
  d_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}(16\pi,10^n\pi)<\varepsilon.
\]

Furstenberg's dense two-parameter orbit does not force this one-parameter
diagonal return. Nothing in T69, its imported T68 arithmetic work, or the
current fixed-pi estimates supplies it. Accordingly, this independent audit
confirms a sound `machine-checked` minimal-premise reduction and explicitly
rejects any claim that V1 itself has been proved.
