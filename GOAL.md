# Persistent research goal

Build and operate a compact, sandboxed, machine-verified research system for the decimal digits of π.

Primary question: does every finite digit string occur in the decimal expansion of π?

The system should preserve a continuously improving verified core while using inexpensive model workers for broad exploration. Raw transcripts and failed sandboxes are disposable; durable intermediate results, negative results, and restartable work belong under `knowledge/pi/`.

Current trusted progress includes a machine-checked BBP series identity and exact forced-orbit infrastructure. The decisive distribution or cancellation step remains open, so no candidate or verified resolution exists.
